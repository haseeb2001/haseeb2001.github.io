Research behind the work

WWW Companion '26 · Dubai, UAE First Author

From Code to Rubrics: A Multimodal Evaluation of LLM Systems for Automated Feedback Generation

Haseeb, M., Amjad, A. I., Amjad, M., & Sheng, V. S. — Companion Proceedings of The ACM Web Conference 2026, April 13–17, Dubai. ACM.

Compared unified vs. multi-agent LLM prompting (Qwen2.5-Coder, GPT-OSS, DeepSeek-R1, Llama-3.3) for automated programming feedback on 57 student submissions. Showed single-role prompts outperform tightly-coupled agent pipelines for introductory tasks; introduced a J1-style thinking judge that substantially improved automated evaluation agreement with human annotators.

BEA 2026 @ ACL · San Diego, CA Presented In Person

Toward Cross-Domain Automated Feedback: A Comparative Evaluation of Open-Source Models across Diverse Student Assessment Types

Haseeb, M., et al. — 21st Workshop on Innovative Use of NLP for Building Educational Applications, ACL 2026.

Evaluated Qwen 3.5 and DeepSeek R1 for automated feedback generation across three educational domains — programming, writing, and mathematics — using unified and multi-agent prompting strategies. Key finding: no single open-source model dominates all domains; DeepSeek R1 excels at structured tasks while Qwen 3.5 leads on open-ended ones; multi-agent prompting actively degrades performance on tasks requiring coherent reasoning chains.
